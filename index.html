BIG DATA PIPELINE

Does credit history improve predictions?

HiveQL summarized credit bureau records, prior applications, installments, credit card activity and POS/cash loans into 17 history features. The join kept one row per application. Spark SQL ran the queries with a Hive metastore in about 142 seconds of logged statement time on one local Ubuntu instance.

What if review time is limited?

A lender can use predicted risk to prioritize extra checks, such as verifying information or asking for clarification. Set a review limit to compare CatBoost and XGBoost at the same workload.

Study notes

The application-only and Hive-history studies share a stratified 60/20/20 split. Age and recorded gender are reserved for auditing. Historical records were restricted to earlier days or months. Kaggle competition test rows have no outcome labels, so they were excluded from evaluation. Predictions and audit results on this site come from the Hive-history experiment.
